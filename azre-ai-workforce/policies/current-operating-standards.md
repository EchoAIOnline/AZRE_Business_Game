# AZRE Current Operating Standards

Approved by: Ashari Zakar, CEO
Effective: October 14, 2026
Authority: Current CEO-approved operating policy. This policy supersedes contradictory Obsidian guidance on the subjects below. Other subjects remain subject to their existing approvals and review flags.

## Acquisition strategy and preliminary screening

The primary focus is on-market single-family properties in Metro Atlanta, particularly Sandy Springs, Dunwoody, Brookhaven, and Buckhead.

Renovations: ARV × 40%.
Teardowns/new construction: ARV × 20%–25%.

These percentages determine preliminary acquisition targets. They are not complete construction budgets or substitutes for transaction-level underwriting.

## SureCash Offer default contract terms

- Earnest money deposit: 1%.
- Due diligence: seven days.
- Closing: 30 days.
- Purchase condition: as-is.

These are defaults, not inflexible requirements for every negotiated transaction. No universal earnest-money delivery deadline or business-day interpretation is established here; verify negotiated contract terms rather than importing an older conflicting deadline. The prior listing-price-minus-$70,000 anchor is not established as the current standard by this policy.

## Departmental communication responsibilities

Only the Acquisitions Manager contacts listing agents for MLS-listed properties.
The Marketing Manager handles off-market seller outreach and buyer marketing.
The Dispositions Manager handles direct buyer communication and negotiations.

These responsibilities do not authorize this read-only application to send communications or make commitments. Consequential actions require separate human authorization.

## DealDesk Queue Status and Pipeline Status

A property belongs In Queue when meaningful seller or agent communication has not occurred. Once communication occurs, it moves In Pipeline.

Queue Status is queueStatus; Pipeline Status is offerDecision and describes offer progress separately. An assigned listing agent does not prove communication. A sent-message flag alone does not establish meaningful communication, delivery or response. Conflicts between stored labels and communication evidence require review; this application must not modify production records.

## Knowledge authority and access

Corporate knowledge belongs to the AZRE AI Workforce platform, not an individual agent. All agents use a common service with server-assigned departmental access. Operations Manager has broader access to nonconfidential departmental procedures for coordination and oversight. Acquisitions may access acquisition procedures, comparable-sale rules and underwriting policies. Dispositions may access buyer qualification and disposition workflows. Missing procedures must be acknowledged rather than invented.

Private lender information and confidential financial or personal records remain denied unless specifically authorized. Obsidian source notes remain read-only. Contradictory notes are retained and flagged for review; they do not override this approved policy. Live DealDesk governs current operational records.

## Approved comparable-sale requirements — Phase 1.3A

Renovations: find up to three verified closed, on-market retail MLS sales of renovated properties, prioritizing the current calendar year. Match the subject's bedrooms and bathrooms, approximately ±300 square feet, and within one mile.

New construction: find up to three verified closed, on-market retail MLS sales of single-family detached spec-infill properties within one mile. Prioritize the current calendar year, then the rolling 12-month period. If fewer than three qualifying sales exist, report only those available. Stored DealDesk comparable slots without supporting sale, source, condition, date and distance evidence remain candidates, not independently verified sales. No authorized independent MLS sales feed is currently configured in this application; missing verification must be disclosed.

These requirements were approved by the CEO in the Phase 1.3A specification. The current application remains read-only; listing-agent communication responsibilities do not grant outbound permission.
